Common Area Redevelopment

This project for Embassy in Chennai, managed by JLL, involved high-precision civil works for common area redevelopment. The project covered 1,50,000 Sq. Ft of landscape and lobby areas, focused on enhancing the tenant experience through modern structural upgrades.

Executing heavy civil work in an active business park environment required stringent safety protocols and a phased approach to ensure continuous operation for all occupants.
