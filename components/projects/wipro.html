Pan-India Refurbishment

A massive national-scale project involving the technical refurbishment of toilet blocks and sanitary facilities across Wipro's Pan-India campuses. Spanning a cumulative area of 4 Lakhs Sq. Ft, this project required rigorous coordination and rapid execution.

Each location was completed within a tight 45-day window, including demolition, MEP upgrades, waterproofing, and high-end finishing, with teams of 50+ people per site.

Technical Challenges

  • ● Multi-site logistics and coordination across India
  • ● Zero-leakage waterproofing solutions with 5-year warranties
  • ● Integrated sensor-based sanitaryware installation
  • ● Rapid turnover to minimize facility downtime
